Develops spectral embedding via the graph Laplacian: building a word similarity graph from distributional context, defining the Laplacian and its quadratic form, and solving the generalized eigenvalue problem to obtain low-dimensional word vectors. Connects count-based co-occurrence statistics to vector space geometry.

Word2Vec
Develops Word2Vec as the predictive counterpart to the spectral embeddings of the previous article. Derives both architectures (CBOW and skipgram) as shallow neural networks whose softmax scores inner products of input and output word vectors, and works out the gradient updates that give the training its "pull together / push apart" geometric reading. Develops the intractable-softmax problem and its negative-sampling fix as a logistic binary classifier, then shows how the resulting embeddings acquire the linear analogy structure (king - man + woman ≈ queen) that reshaped representation learning in 2013.
